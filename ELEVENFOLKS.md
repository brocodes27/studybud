# ElevenFolks: The Neural OS for STEM Mastery

**ElevenFolks** is an advanced AI-powered study ecosystem designed specifically for STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) students. It functions as a "Neural Operating System" that centralizes study logic, automates routine academic tasks, and provides a persistent AI companion for deep learning.

## 🚀 Core Philosophy: The Feynman-2 Logic
At the heart of ElevenFolks is the **Feynman Protocol**. Instead of just giving answers, the platform's AI (often referred to as **ATLAS**) acts as a "Simulated Pupil." It challenges students to explain complex concepts in simple terms, identifying logical gaps and forcing true mastery rather than rote memorization.

## 🛠️ Key Components & Features

### 1. ATLAS AI Study Buddy
The core companion that lives within the workspace. ATLAS is not just a chatbot; it's a persistent agent that:
- **Remembers Context**: Recalls your history, specific subjects, and past notes.
- **STEM Specialized**: Capable of solving complex math, physics, and chemistry problems with step-by-step verification.
- **Voice Interactivity**: Powered by high-fidelity voice models (like Vapi/ElevenLabs) for real-time oral examinations and lectures.

### 2. SYOW (Study Your Own Way)
A specialized ingestion engine that allows students to turn any resource into a lesson:
- **Multi-Source**: Ingests PDFs, Web Links, and YouTube videos.
- **Bifurcation**: Automatically breaks down long content into logical "Study Units."
- **Neural Indexing**: Surfaces specific facts and references from the source material during chat or voice lectures.

### 3. Neural Planner & Scheduler
Replaces traditional static schedules with a dynamic, exam-aligned engine:
- **One-Year Roadmaps**: Generates long-term academic trajectories based on exam dates (SAT, AP, JEE, etc.).
- **Daily Mission Control**: Provides specific topics and tasks for the day, tracking progress and adjusting for backlogs.

### 4. Exam Simulators
Adaptive testing environments that mimic real-world exams. These simulators adjust difficulty in real-time based on student performance, providing detailed AI-generated feedback on every mistake.

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Experience
ElevenFolks utilizes a **Neo-Brutalist** design system—characterized by bold borders, high-contrast colors (often neon accents on dark or paper backgrounds), and heavy typography. This "Command Center" aesthetic is designed to make studying feel like a high-stakes, high-reward technical operation.
