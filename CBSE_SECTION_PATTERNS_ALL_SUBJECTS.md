## Key Observations

### Common Pattern Elements:
1. **MCQs**: Present in all subjects (typically 16-20 questions)
2. **VSA (Very Short Answer)**: 2-mark questions (4-7 questions)
3. **SA (Short Answer)**: 3-mark questions (5-7 questions)
4. **LA (Long Answer)**: 5-mark questions (3-4 questions)
5. **Case-Based/Competency Questions**: 4-mark questions (2-3 questions)
6. **Internal Choice**: Usually in VSA, SA, and LA sections

### Competency-Based Assessment (40%):
- Objective-type questions: 20%
- Short/Long answer questions: 40%
- Case-based questions: 12-15%

### Subject-Specific Features:
- **Map-based questions**: Social Science, Political Science, History, Geography
- **Source-based questions**: History, Political Science
- **Numerical problems**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Accountancy
- **Programming questions**: Computer Science
- **Extract-based questions**: English, Hindi (Literature)

---

## Notes for Implementation

1. **Assertion-Reasoning**: Treated as MCQs (objective type)
2. **Case-Based Questions**: Mapped to short answer with 4 marks each
3. **Map-Based Questions**: Mapped to short answer with marks as specified
4. **Source-Based Questions**: Similar to case-based, typically 4 marks
5. **Extract-Based Questions**: MCQs or short answer depending on format

When generating questions, ensure:
- Questions align with CBSE syllabus chapters
- Difficulty distribution: Easy (30%), Medium (50%), Hard (20%)
- Competency-based questions test application, analysis, evaluation
- Internal choice questions are of similar difficulty
